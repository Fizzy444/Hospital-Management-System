Hospital Management System

1. Patient Registration and Authentication:
Patients should be able to register by providing their name, age, contact details, and other basic information.
The system should generate a unique patient ID for each patient.
Patients should be able to log in and view their profile.

2. Doctor Management:
The hospital should maintain information about doctors, including name, specialization, department, and availability.
Administrators should be able to add, update, and remove doctor details.
Patients should be able to view doctors based on their specialization.

3. Appointment Booking:
Patients should be able to search for available doctors and book appointments.
The system should store the appointment date, time, doctor, and patient details.
Patients should be able to cancel or reschedule their appointments.

4. Patient Medical Records:
Doctors should be able to add consultation details, diagnosis, and prescriptions for patients.
The system should maintain the patient's previous medical records.
Doctors should be able to view a patient's history during a consultation.

5. Medicine and Prescription Management:
Doctors should be able to prescribe medicines to patients.
Prescription information may include medicine name, dosage, duration, and instructions.
Patients should be able to view their current and previous prescriptions.

6. Billing and Payment:
The system should generate bills for consultations, medicines, tests, and other hospital services.
Patients should be able to view their total bill and payment status.
The system should support different payment statuses such as Pending, Paid, and Partially Paid.

7. Room/Bed Management:
The hospital should maintain information about available and occupied rooms or beds.
Patients who require admission should be assigned an available bed.
The system should record admission date, discharge date, room number, and associated charges.

Expected Features to Build:
Patient registration and login
Doctor and department management
Appointment booking and cancellation
Medical record management
Prescription and medicine management
Billing and payment management
Room and bed allocation
